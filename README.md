# Oshin Miranda, PhD

**AI Scientist, Healthcare** | Machine learning, clinical NLP, and real-world evidence

I develop machine learning solutions that turn real-world healthcare data into decision-ready insight. My work spans multimodal data, including electronic medical records, claims, and clinical text, with a focus on explainable models for risk prediction, treatment patterns, and healthcare disparities, particularly in mental health and high-risk populations.

## Background

- PhD in Pharmaceutical Sciences (AI/ML concentration), University of Pittsburgh
- 18+ peer-reviewed publications in clinical AI, NLP, and pharmacoepidemiology
- Real-world data experience: health-system EHR, CMS Medicare claims (Parts A, C, D), and the All of Us Research Program
- Methods: predictive modeling, deep learning, clinical NLP, LLM fine-tuning and evaluation, causal inference for observational data

## Featured Projects

| Project | Summary |
|---|---|
| [Clinical Note Classifier](https://github.com/oshinmiranda26/clinical-note-classifier) | Benchmarks TF-IDF with logistic regression against fine-tuned Bio_ClinicalBERT and a LoRA-tuned 0.5B LLM for clinical note classification, with controlled experiments and error analysis; the simplest model won. [Live demo](https://clinical-note-classifier.streamlit.app). |
| [RDoC Clinical PTSD](https://github.com/oshinmiranda26/RDoC_Clinical_PTSD) | NLP workflow for identifying Research Domain Criteria in clinical notes of patients with PTSD; code accompanying a peer-reviewed research study. |
| [DeepBiomarker](https://github.com/oshinmiranda26/DeepBiomarker) | From-scratch reimplementation of my published DeepBiomarker approach on synthetic data: GRU over visit histories with SDoH and a polygenic risk score; perturbation-based explanations recovered all 13 planted risk factors. |
| [Lung Cancer Multi-Omics](https://github.com/oshinmiranda26/lung-cancer-genomics) | Survival analysis of TCGA lung adenocarcinoma integrating clinical data, mutations, copy number, and gene expression; validated against known biology, with STK11 and proliferation as robust prognostic factors. |
| [Medicare Claims RWE](https://github.com/oshinmiranda26/medicare-claims-rwe) | Real-world evidence pipeline built from raw Medicare claims files in SQL: data-quality checks, continuous enrollment, a new-diagnosis cohort with attrition table, and a Charlson index computed from raw ICD-9 codes. |
| [NeuroRisk Clinical Risk Intelligence](https://github.com/oshinmiranda26/NeuroRisk-Clinical-Risk-Intelligence) | Evaluated retrieval-augmented QA over synthetic mental health records: six retrievers benchmarked against ground truth, cited answers, and a [live demo](https://neurorisk-clinical.streamlit.app). |
